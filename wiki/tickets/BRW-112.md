# BRW-112 Empty launchpad after the upgrade

**Client:** Brackenfield Waste · **Status:** In progress · **Systems:** BD1 → BQ1 → BP1

## Ask
After the weekend upgrade the dispatch team opened the launchpad and saw an empty home page. No tiles, and app search found nothing they could open.

## What was found
- Authorizations were fine: a test user with the dispatch role could start the apps directly by URL intent.
- [[S_TCODE]] and [[S_SERVICE]] checks passed in STAUTHTRACE.
- The upgrade had switched the launchpad to **spaces and pages**. The dispatch role still carried its catalogs and an old classic group, but no space. In spaces mode a catalog without a space shows nothing. See [[fiori-launchpad-content]].

## What was built
One space per business role, each with a single page laid out in the same sections as the old group. The spaces went into the role menus in PFCG next to the catalogs, so the existing role assignments did the rest. Caches cleared with `/UI2/INVALIDATE_CLIENT_CACHES` after each transport.

## Decisions
- Spaces, not switching the old mode back on. The classic home page is on its way out; doing it once now beats doing it under pressure later.
- Same sections as the old groups, so the dispatchers find their tiles where they left them.
- The space goes in the **same role** as the catalog. Separate "layout roles" were discussed and dropped: two roles to keep in step for every change. See [[pfcg-role-design]].

## Still open
Spaces for the two remaining roles, then a test session with Jonas's team in BQ1.
