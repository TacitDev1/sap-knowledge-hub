# OSU-207 Meter readers locked out

**Client:** Osterby Utilities · **Status:** Resolved · **Systems:** BQ1 → BP1

## Ask
On the morning after the new password rules went live in BP1, about 40 meter readers were locked and could not sync their field tablets.

## What was found
- The rules themselves were correct and agreed with the security officer. See [[logon-and-password-rules]].
- `RSUSR006` showed five failed logons per user within about a minute of each other, all before the users had even picked up their tablets.
- The security audit log showed the failed logons coming from the tablets, not from the users' desktops.
- The field app stores the password and retries it in the background. With `login/password_compliance_to_current_policy` on, the old password needed a change the app could not offer, so each retry failed until the lock limit was reached.

## What was done
1. Unlocked the users with SU10 so the depots could work.
2. Device team pushed a profile that clears the stored credentials; users sign in once and set a new password on the tablet.
3. `RSUSR006` checked each morning until every depot was done.

## Decisions
- No automatic midnight unlock (`login/failed_user_auto_unlock` stays 0). It would have hidden the cause and weakens the lock for everyone.
- Rules unchanged. The fix belonged on the devices.

## Lessons
Before tightening password rules, list every device, job and interface that stores a password. Those are the accounts that lock first.
